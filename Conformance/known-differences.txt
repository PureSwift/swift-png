meta-palette-hist.png The reference build refuses hIST wherever it appears in the stream, before or after PLTE and with or without other chunks between, reporting "out of place" or "invalid". Since it never accepts the chunk, its own png_get_hIST can never report anything, which makes the behaviour a defect rather than a rule. This library accepts a histogram that follows the palette it is sized against, as the format defines.
